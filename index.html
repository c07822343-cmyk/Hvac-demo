04The System

How a house stays cool.

One loop, four movements.

  1. 01

    Heat enters

    Sun, humidity and infiltration load the envelope from morning to dusk.

  2. 02

    The system conditions

    Return air is drawn across a cold coil; the heat is carried outside and released at the condenser.

  3. 03

    Cool air moves

    Supply ducts deliver cooled, dehumidified air to every room, quietly.

  4. 04

    Comfort is restored

    The thermostat holds the setpoint. The house settles. The system fades out.

Simplified diagram — every home is different. We explain yours before we touch it.
